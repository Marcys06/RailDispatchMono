# 0.2.0 — Operational railway simulation

## Scope

0.2.0 connects the existing timetable, station, signal and block systems into one operational runtime while preserving the player's responsibility for railway infrastructure.

## Locomotive timetable

- locomotives can own an independent `LocomotiveSchedule`;
- wagon timetables remain unchanged and continue to belong to wagons;
- timetable points contain expected arrival and required departure times;
- an automatically operated locomotive waits at a scheduled station until its required departure time;
- actual arrival is recorded against the timetable and produces runtime delay;
- the timetable remains cyclic.

## Dispatcher and blocks

- `RailwayDispatcher` arbitrates access to the next connected block;
- arbitration is first-come-first-served through the simulation update order;
- an occupied or reserved block belonging to another train prevents automatic movement into that block;
- reservations are released when the train enters the reserved block;
- the dispatcher does not set switches and does not change signal aspects;
- existing signals remain the player's infrastructure-control mechanism;
- existing block occupancy remains authoritative for physical train presence.

## Player role

Automatic timetable execution does not automate the whole railway. The player remains responsible for:

- setting junctions/switches;
- setting and correcting signal aspects;
- resolving situations where a train cannot obtain the required path;
- manual shunting and coupling/decoupling;
- taking manual control of a train when required.

F6/manual shunting and RadioStop semantics remain unchanged.

## Passengers and stations

The existing passenger ownership model is preserved: passengers riding a train remain owned by their concrete wagon. Existing station service and dwell processing remain responsible for boarding/alighting. No automatic passenger transfer system is introduced by 0.2.0.

## Persistence

`TrainSchedule` schema is now version 2 and can persist an optional locomotive timetable together with wagon schedule definitions. Backward compatibility with old saves is not a 0.2.0 requirement.

## Documentation

The architecture and current-state documentation must describe locomotive timetable ownership, runtime execution, dispatcher/block arbitration and the preserved player-control boundaries.

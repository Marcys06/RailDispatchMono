# 0.1.5pre — Final pre-release documentation

**Data:** 2026-09-03

`0.1.5pre` is the final planned state of the 0.1.5 development cycle. The release is closed at this pre-release version; further work belongs to a later version.

## F7 — travel-direction change

F7 is a travel-direction change, not a physical reversal of the consist.

For:

```text
Composition.Vehicles = [L][A][B][C]
```

F7 guarantees:

- `Composition.Vehicles` remains unchanged.
- Vehicle world positions are unchanged at the exact reversal moment.
- Vehicle-to-vehicle distances remain unchanged.
- The train `Direction` changes to the opposite cardinal direction.
- `Speed` remains a non-negative magnitude.
- F7 is accepted only when the train is stopped (`0 km/h`).
- The locomotive is not teleported to the opposite end.
- No vehicle is moved or reordered by F7.
- Vehicle graphics are not rotated as a separate F7 operation.

Example:

```text
Before:

[L][A][B][C]  ->

After F7:

[L][A][B][C]  <-
```

Subsequent movement translates the complete formation in the new direction.

## Curves and vehicle orientation

The consist follows the travelled trajectory through curves. Each vehicle uses the historical point on the train trajectory corresponding to its physical distance behind the head.

The position and rotation of a vehicle are derived from the same point on that trajectory. Therefore vehicles enter and leave a curve sequentially instead of rotating simultaneously with the head.

The head uses the exact tangent of the active curve arc. Following vehicles derive their orientation from the local trajectory tangent. When there is not yet sufficient movement history, the formation uses its rigid straight-track offsets and the current cardinal direction until the required trajectory history exists.

This preserves the rigid consist spacing while allowing the formation to occupy the actual path of the track through a curve.

## Vehicle positioning and spawn

The train maintains fixed vehicle offsets for rigid straight-track movement. Offsets are initialized from the initial travel direction and the lengths of the vehicles in the composition.

The first vehicle is placed at the train reference position. Each following vehicle is placed behind the preceding formation according to the existing vehicle-length spacing rule. A complete consist therefore no longer collapses all vehicles into one grid coordinate at spawn.

Changing direction does not rebuild, mirror or invert these offsets.

## Coupling and decoupling

The rigid coupling model is authoritative in `CouplingService`.

- Runtime connections are between concrete vehicles and concrete `VehicleEnd` values.
- Physical compatibility is determined by `CouplingSpecification` / `CouplerType`.
- Pre-built adjacent vehicles can establish compatible runtime couplings automatically.
- Coupling operates on outer train boundaries and preserves vehicle order.
- `X` decouples the wagon under the cursor and prefers its rear connection when both ends are connected.
- Decoupling is allowed only below `6 km/h`.
- A detached section becomes a separate stopped train while preserving its internal vehicle order and runtime connections.

## Shunting controls

- `C` — couple with the nearest valid boundary candidate.
- `X` — decouple the wagon under the cursor.
- `F6` — manual shunting toward `3 km/h` while held over a train.
- Coupling uses a fixed `6 km/h` shunting limit.
- The former configurable `F6/F7/F8` coupling-speed profiles are removed. F7 is reserved for train direction reversal.

`F6` manual shunting bypasses the automatic RadioStop/collision stop path for the targeted train while the command is held. Other trains continue through normal safety processing.

## Safety and signalling

RadioStop remains an independent safety mechanism. Coupling and decoupling reset the affected train signal state so stale signal restrictions are not carried across composition changes.

Signal speed handling remains part of the normal train movement path; coupling itself uses the fixed `6 km/h` shunting limit.

## Persistence

The persisted reversal state remains represented by `IsReversed`. Loading restores the saved travel-direction state without reconstructing or reordering the physical vehicle list.

## Deliberate scope boundary

`0.1.5pre` remains a rigid consist model. It does not attempt to implement slack action, impact forces, coupling delay, brake-pipe propagation or full longitudinal vehicle dynamics.

The final milestone does include curve trajectory following and per-vehicle curve orientation. Curves are therefore no longer a deferred part of the implemented movement model.

## Verification boundary

The final curve/F7 behavior was verified in the running game by the developer. The repository does not contain a dedicated Core test project.

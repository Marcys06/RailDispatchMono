# 0.1.5pre — Train direction reversal rebuild

**Data:** 2026-09-03

## F7 contract

F7 is a travel-direction change, not a consist reversal.

For a composition:

```text
Composition.Vehicles = [L][A][B][C]
```

F7 guarantees:

- `Composition.Vehicles` is unchanged.
- Every vehicle keeps its world position at the exact reversal moment.
- Vehicle-to-vehicle distances are unchanged.
- The train `Direction` changes to the opposite cardinal direction.
- `Speed` remains a non-negative magnitude and F7 is accepted only at 0 km/h.
- No vehicle is moved to the opposite end of the consist.
- No vehicle order is changed.

Example:

```text
Before:

[L][A][B][C]  ->

After F7:

[L][A][B][C]  <-
```

The following movement then translates the complete rigid formation in the new direction.

## Vehicle positioning

Vehicle world positions are represented as offsets from the train's physical reference position. The offsets are initialized from the initial travel direction and vehicle lengths. Changing `Direction` alone does not rebuild or invert those offsets.

This prevents the previous failure mode where changing direction caused a composition to appear as `[C][B][A][L]` or caused all vehicles to collapse onto one spawn coordinate.

## Spawn

A train created from a complete composition now assigns distinct initial positions to successive vehicles using the existing vehicle-length spacing rule. The first vehicle is at the train position; each following vehicle is placed behind the preceding formation along the initial travel direction.

## Scope

This milestone intentionally targets straight-track behavior. Curve-specific reversal is deferred until the straight-track model is stable.

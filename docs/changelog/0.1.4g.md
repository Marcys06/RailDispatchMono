# 0.1.4g — Rolling stock presentation and locomotive power

**Data:** 2026-09-03

## Rolling stock visuals

- Added differentiated top-down presentation for locomotives and wagons in `TrainRenderer`.
- Electric locomotives render red.
- Diesel locomotives render black.
- Locomotives show centered white short labels: `EP07`, `EU200`, `SU42`.
- Passenger coaches render in distinct blue shades and show centered white labels: `1KL`, `2KL`, `3KL`.
- Labels are rotated with the vehicle and normalized so they remain readable when a train travels in either direction.
- The renderer receives the existing `Arial24` font from `GameplayScreen`; no additional UI/desktop is introduced.

## Locomotive power

- Added `LocomotiveParameters : VehicleParameters` with `PowerMW`.
- Added locomotive power to the rolling-stock catalogue:
  - EP07 — 2.0 MW;
  - EU200 — 5.5 MW;
  - SU42 — 1.2 MW.
- Added short display labels to locomotive and wagon definitions/instances.

## Power-dependent Vmax

The effective consist Vmax is now calculated from locomotive power and total physical consist mass. The base locomotive Vmax is retained until the supported mass is exceeded.

Current gameplay model:

`supportedMass = PowerMW / 0.006`

`VmaxMultiplier = 1`, when `totalMass <= supportedMass`.

`VmaxMultiplier = (supportedMass / totalMass)^0.55`, when `totalMass > supportedMass`.

`effectiveVmax = baseLocomotiveVmax * VmaxMultiplier`, additionally limited by the slowest wagon Vmax.

Calibration with 40 t passenger coaches:

- EU200: 5.5 MW, 84 t locomotive + 10 wagons = 484 t total → 200 km/h.
- SU42: 1.2 MW, 74 t locomotive + 5 wagons = 274 t total → approximately 75 km/h.
- SU42: 1.2 MW, 74 t locomotive + 10 wagons = 474 t total → approximately 55 km/h.

All three passenger coach variants now permit 200 km/h at the catalogue level so the EU200 calibration is governed by locomotive power rather than an artificial wagon Vmax cap.

## Interaction with 0.1.4f

- The non-linear mass effect on acceleration/braking remains unchanged: `1 / (totalMass / locomotiveMass)^1.30`.
- Power affects Vmax only; it does not replace or modify the 0.1.4f acceleration/braking mass model.
- Depot remains the Myra-based composition builder and continues to create trains through `TrainManager.CreateTrainFromComposition()`.
